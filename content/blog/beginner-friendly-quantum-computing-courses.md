Quantum computing is one of the fastest-growing fields in technology today. Governments, research labs, startups, and large technology companies are investing heavily in quantum research and development.

The challenge is that many people interested in the field don't know where to begin.

A lot of learning resources assume prior knowledge of physics or advanced mathematics, making the subject feel more intimidating than it needs to be.

Fortunately, there are now several beginner-friendly courses that make quantum computing more approachable. Whether you're a student, a professional, or simply curious about emerging technologies, these courses can help you take your first step.

## 1. Practical Quantum Computing with Qiskit for Beginners (Coursera)

This [beginner-level Coursera course](https://www.coursera.org/learn/packt-beginners-guide-to-practical-quantum-computing-with-ibm-qiskit-w6mos) focuses on practical implementation using Qiskit. It introduces qubits, quantum gates, circuits, and simulations while helping learners gain hands-on experience with quantum programming.

**Best for:**

- Learners interested in coding
- Developers looking to explore quantum programming
- People familiar with Qiskit concepts

**Price: ~$30**

## 2. QuantumX School: Introduction to Quantum Computing

[QuantumX School's 1-month live cohort](https://quantumx.school/apply) is designed specifically for beginners who want a structured and interactive introduction to quantum computing.

The course covers foundational mathematics, key quantum computing concepts, quantum technologies, Qiskit, basic quantum algorithms, and hands-on programming demonstrations. Unlike many self-paced courses, learners participate in live sessions, assignments, discussions, and a dedicated Discord community.

**Best for:**

- Students and recent graduates
- Professionals exploring future technologies
- Learners who prefer live guidance and community learning
- People looking for course certificates for career progression

**Price: ~$21**

## 3. IBM Quantum Learning

[IBM Quantum Learning](https://learning.quantum.ibm.com/) is one of the most popular starting points for beginners. It offers structured learning paths, tutorials, and hands-on exercises using Qiskit, IBM's quantum computing framework. Learners can build and run quantum circuits while gaining a practical understanding of core concepts.

**Best for:**

- Absolute beginners
- Developers interested in Qiskit
- Self-paced learners

While the content is strong, much of it revolves around IBM's quantum ecosystem rather than providing a wider view of different quantum technologies and platforms.

**Price: Free**

## 4. Quantum Computing Fundamentals by MIT xPRO

[MIT xPRO's Quantum Computing Fundamentals program](https://learn-xpro.mit.edu/quantum-computing) is designed for professionals, engineers, and technology leaders looking to understand the principles and potential of quantum computing. The course introduces foundational concepts such as qubits, superposition, entanglement, quantum algorithms, and the broader quantum ecosystem while exploring real-world applications and industry trends.

Unlike many coding-focused beginner courses, this program places greater emphasis on understanding the technology, its business implications, and its future impact across industries.

**Best for:**

- Technology leaders and decision-makers
- Engineers looking to understand quantum computing concepts
- Learners interested in industry applications and future trends

**Price: ~$2,500**

## 5. The Complete Quantum Computing Course by Codestars (Udemy)

[This course](https://www.udemy.com/course/quantum-computers) offers a practical introduction to quantum computing using Python and Qiskit. Learners are guided through the fundamentals of quantum mechanics, quantum circuits, quantum gates, and algorithms while building and executing their own quantum programs. The course focuses heavily on implementation, making it a good choice for those who prefer learning by doing.

**Best for:**

- Python developers
- Beginners interested in quantum programming
- Learners looking for a hands-on introduction to Qiskit and quantum circuits

**Price: ~$5**

## Which course should you choose?

The best course depends on how you prefer to learn.

If you enjoy self-paced learning and experimentation, IBM Quantum Learning and Coursera offer excellent resources.

If you learn better through live interaction, structured guidance, assignments, and community support, cohort-based programs such as [QuantumX School](https://quantumx.school/apply) can provide a more engaging learning experience.

The most important thing is starting. If you want a plan to wrap around whichever course you pick, our [practical 90-day path for beginners](/blog/how-beginners-can-get-into-quantum-computing/) lays out what to learn week by week.

Quantum computing may seem intimidating at first, but every researcher, engineer, and developer in the field started by learning the basics.
